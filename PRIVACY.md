# Privacy Policy

Minoo collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no third parties of any kind.

Your notes are plain Markdown files on your own device. If you pair two of your own devices, Minoo sends notes directly between them over your local network, never through a server.

Minoo is open source under the [MIT licence](https://github.com/ScepticDope/Minoo/blob/main/LICENCE), so every claim here can be checked in the code.

Questions can be raised at [github.com/ScepticDope/Minoo/issues](https://github.com/ScepticDope/Minoo/issues).
